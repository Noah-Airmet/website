Current quality gates

Imported is not finished. A landed markdown file means the vault has a record. It does not mean the text was collated against the scan, and it does not mean the metadata source link is authoritative.

Heavyweight is the archival bar. The blue count only moves when a talk carries heavyweight_reviewed_by: source URL verified, metadata proved, OCR/layout artifacts corrected, and uncertainty recorded instead of guessed.

Carruth is a separate source workstream. CR 100 912 is not a conference board; it is an 848-entry CHL ledger of LaJean Purcell Carruth shorthand transcriptions that can become preferred witnesses after OCR/fetch and match review.

Source authority matters. Heavyweight agents must prefer official Church, Church History Library, or Joseph Smith Papers links. IA, Deseret News, BYU mirrors, and hobbyist transcripts are witnesses/helpers unless no institutional source can be proved.

Full specs live in the vault: docs/REVIEW-WORKFLOW.md, docs/proofreading-cleanup-workflow.md, docs/carruth-ocr-ingestion.md, docs/journal-of-discourses-ingestion.md, and schema/frontmatter.md.

Historical outliers — cancellations, disruptions & off-cycle conferences

Not every General Conference happened on the modern twice-a-year, April/October schedule. Some were cancelled, postponed, closed to the public, moved, or never held at all. These are tracked in indexes/conference-calendar.md and drive how talks are dated and categorized — a conference that didn't happen has no talks to ingest, and shows as ✕Not held on the boards below rather than as completed.

1830
Off-cycle
First two conferences, held in June and September · Fayette, New York
The Church's first general conferences were held June 9 and September 26, 1830 — the twice-a-year April/October rhythm did not yet exist.
1831-1839
Off-cycle
Irregular conference schedule
Conferences were called "from time to time" — some years had one, others several, held in Kirtland, Missouri, Far West, and Commerce. See progress/early-restoration.md for the individual dates.
1842
Disrupted
General conferences temporarily suspended · Nauvoo, Illinois
Joseph Smith suspended general conferences until the Nauvoo Temple could be built; local special conferences continued, but the regular fall general conference was not held.
1846
None held
No general conferences held — Nauvoo exodus
With the Saints mid-exodus from Nauvoo, neither the April nor the October 1846 general conference was held.
1847
Off-cycle
No October conference; December sustaining conference instead · Council Bluffs (Kanesville), Iowa
There was no October 1847 conference. A special conference held December 25-27, 1847 at Council Bluffs (Kanesville) sustained Brigham Young as President of the Church. (The reorganization of the First Presidency by the Twelve is often dated Dec. 5, 1847 — worth confirming.)
1850
Off-cycle
Fall conference moved to September · Salt Lake City
The fall conference was held September 6-8, 1850 (not October) so missionaries could depart before winter.
1851
Off-cycle
Fall conference moved to September · Salt Lake City
The fall conference was held September 7-10, 1851 (not October). Only the Sixth General Epistle survives; no individual sermon transcripts exist.
1854
Off-cycle
Extra June special conference · Salt Lake City
In addition to the regular April and October conferences, a special conference was held June 27-28, 1854.
1858
Off-cycle
Extra November special conference · Salt Lake City
A special conference was held November 13-14, 1858, in addition to the regular April and October sessions.
1870
Off-cycle
Extra May special conference · Salt Lake City
A special conference was held May 5-8, 1870, in addition to the regular April and October sessions.
1871
Off-cycle
Extra October special conference · Salt Lake City
A special conference was held October 5, 1871, immediately before the regular October 6-8 general conference.
1874
Off-cycle
Extra May special conference · Salt Lake City
A special conference was held May 7-9, 1874, in addition to the regular April and October sessions.
1877
Relocated
April conference held in St. George · St. George, Utah
The April 1877 conference was held in St. George, Utah, in connection with the dedication of the St. George Temple.
1885
Relocated
Both conferences held in Logan · Logan, Utah
Both the April and October 1885 conferences were held in Logan, Utah, as Church leaders avoided Salt Lake City during the federal anti-polygamy raids.
1886
Relocated
Conferences held in Provo and Coalville · Provo & Coalville, Utah
The April 1886 conference was held in Provo and the October conference in Coalville, Utah, during the federal anti-polygamy raids.
1887
Relocated
April conference held in Provo · Provo, Utah
The April 1887 conference was held in Provo, Utah, during the federal anti-polygamy raids; the October conference returned to Salt Lake City.
1919
Disrupted
April conference postponed to June — influenza pandemic · Salt Lake City
The April 1919 conference was postponed to June because of the influenza pandemic; Heber J. Grant was sustained as President of the Church in a solemn assembly.
1942
Disrupted
Conference closed to the public — World War II · Salt Lake City
The April 1942 conference was closed to the public because of World War II; attendance was limited to General Authorities and local priesthood leaders and it was broadcast by radio. (Note — attendance was restricted across 1942-1945; only 1942 is recorded here so far. The remaining WWII years still need to be added.)
1957
Cancelled
October conference cancelled — Asian flu · Salt Lake City
The October 1957 general conference was cancelled because of the Asian flu (H2N2) epidemic. No Conference Report was published and no talks exist — this is why a search for October 1957 returns nothing.
2020
Disrupted
Conferences held without public attendance — COVID-19 · Salt Lake City
The April and October 2020 conferences were held without public attendance because of the COVID-19 pandemic, in a small auditorium at Church headquarters; only speakers attended.
